🌍 The Eight Planets

☿️

Mercury

Sun ke sabse paas ka planet. Yeh Solar System ka sabse chhota planet hai.

🟡

Venus

Solar System ka sabse garam planet. Iska atmosphere bahut dense hai.

🌍

Earth

Hamara ghar! Abhi tak jeevan ke liye jaana jaane wala ekmatra planet.

🔴

Mars

Isse Red Planet kehte hain. Scientists yahan purane paani ke saboot dhoondhte hain.

🟠

Jupiter

Solar System ka sabse bada planet. Is par ek vishal storm hai.

🪐

Saturn

Apne sundar rings ke liye famous gas giant planet.

🔵

Uranus

Ek ice giant jo apni side par jhuka hua ghoomta hai.

🌊

Neptune

Sun se sabse door ka officially recognized planet.

🌌 Why Is Space Amazing?

Space mein stars, galaxies, planets aur bahut si mysteries hain. Astronomy humein Universe ko samajhne mein help karti hai.
